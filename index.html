Sound Designer

I am a sound designer focused on creating immersive sonic environments for film and advertising. My work explores texture, atmosphere, and emotional storytelling through sound.

I approach each project as a narrative space: shaping ambiences, designing impactful details, and building soundscapes that support the visual world without overpowering it. Whether cinematic or commercial, my goal is always the same, to make sound feel alive.

This portfolio presents a selection of film excerpts and advertising projects that reflect my approach to sound design: precise, expressive, and driven by storytelling.
